# Safe TvT

A reference for a cautious Terran-versus-Terran opener that attacks once Stimpack is ready. Adjust it dynamically according to the game situation.

## Summary

Double gas, a few Reapers and Hellions, 1 defensive Cyclone, then 2 Ravens. After that, Marines, Tanks, and Vikings, with Stimpack before the attack. Workers keep growing, toward about 66.

## Details

* Opening: Train SCVs to 14 before the 1st Supply Depot, to 15 before that depot finishes, and to 19 before the Barracks finishes. Then 1 Refinery, the Barracks, a 2nd Refinery at 17 supply, a Factory, and a 2nd Supply Depot. Make about 3 Reapers and 2 Hellions for the early map, and 1 Cyclone as the defensive tech unit.
* Ravens: Expand to the natural with priority. A 3rd Supply Depot, a Starport, a Factory Tech Lab, and a Barracks Tech Lab. Produce 2 Ravens before committing the Starport to anything else. Morph Orbitals after the 1st Barracks.
* Transition: A 3rd Command Center around 4:20. After the Ravens, a Reactor on the Starport and Vikings, about 6 to start, then Medivacs and a small Liberator count. Barracks to 3, then 5, with Reactors. 2 Engineering Bays, a 2nd Factory with a Tech Lab, and 6 Refineries once 5 Barracks exist and gas is below 200.
* Upgrades: Stimpack first, then infantry weapons and armor, Combat Shield, and the vehicle and ship weapon upgrades.
* Economy: Workers to 22, then keep going toward about 66.
* Army: Marines are the body, Tanks come from the Tech Lab Factories, Vikings contest air.
* Attack: Move out when Stimpack is complete, and keep reinforcing that army. Stimpack is the timing.
