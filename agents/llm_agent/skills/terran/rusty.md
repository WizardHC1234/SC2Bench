# Marine–Tank Timing

## Strategy

Open gas first, expand after the first Marine and use 2 Hellions as a short bridge into Siege Tanks. The mature army is Marine–Tank–Medivac, with Vikings, Ravens and static detection added only when the enemy composition requires them.

## Development

- Build a Supply Depot around 13 supply, take the first Refinery around 16, then add the Barracks and a second Depot. Morph the Orbital when the Barracks finishes.
- Produce the first Marine, then take the natural Command Center. Maintain SCVs toward about 44 workers.
- Add a Factory and make no more than 2 Hellions before its Tech Lab is ready. Once the first Siege Tank exists, add a second Factory and reach 4 total Refineries.
- Scale to 5 Barracks and add a Starport. Use Barracks Reactors for Marines, a Tech Lab for Combat Shield, Factory Tech Labs for tanks, and the Starport for Medivacs.
- Take a third base after the main production setup is operating.

## Production and adaptation

- Work toward a long-term ceiling of about 20 Siege Tanks, a large Marine screen and up to 6 Medivacs.
- Add Vikings in stages when enemy air warrants them: begin with 1–3 and grow toward 10 only against a substantial air force.
- Against Dark Templar, Banshees or other cloaked threats, add an Engineering Bay, Missile Turrets and up to 2 Ravens.

## Combat

- Move out with a gathered Marine–Tank force rather than using the 2 early Hellions as the main attack.
- Maintain the Marine screen, keep Medivacs with the bio force and reinforce a favorable front. Rebuild the protective layers before recommitting after a poor fight.

## Attack timing

- The 6 Tanks are the slow part of this composition, even after the second Factory is added. With continuous Tank and Marine production, the first complete force should normally form around 6:30–8:00.
- Do not turn the first 2 Hellions into the main attack. Begin the first Marine–Tank timing with about 24 Marines, 6 Siege Tanks and 2 Medivacs; available Ravens or Vikings may accompany it when their targets are present.
- Reinforce while at least 16 Marines and 3 Tanks remain. After heavier losses, retreat and rebuild that minimum protective core before another frontal push.
