# Lurker Macro

## Strategy

Develop from Zergling–Roach into Hydralisk and finally Lurker while expanding aggressively. Each new technology layer supplements the existing army rather than replacing it immediately.

## Development

- Take the natural around 16 supply, the first Extractor around 20, and the third Hatchery near 3:00. Add a fourth around 40 workers.
- Grow the Drone economy steadily toward a late ceiling near 80 when bases remain safe and mineral lines can use them.
- Build the Spawning Pool and Roach Warren, then reach Lair, Hydralisk Den and Lurker Den in that order.
- Start with 1 Extractor around 20 supply. During the Roach stage, reach 2 Extractors near 25 workers, 3 near 40 and 4 near 50; add the sixth around 1,000 minerals and the eighth around 2,000 minerals if the Hydralisk transition has not started.
- Once the Hydralisk Den is ready, use the heavier gas schedule instead: reach 2/3/4/5/6/7/8 Extractors at roughly 20/30/40/50/60/65/70 workers. Maintain Overlords ahead of larva-heavy production.
- Begin with about 2 Queens and grow toward 4; keep roughly 2 Overseers once detection is needed.

## Production

- Start with 4 Zerglings and continue adding them as the mineral-heavy frontline.
- Before Hydralisks, keep the Roach count around half the Zergling count.
- After the Hydralisk Den, aim for slightly more Hydralisks than Roaches.
- After the Lurker Den, add about 1 Lurker for every 3 Hydralisks, with one extra Lurker as the transition begins.
- Research Zergling speed once about 90 gas is available.

## Combat

- Use Zerglings and Roaches to protect Hydralisks and Lurkers during the transition.
- Gather the mixed army before attacking. On defense, keep Lurkers in the defended area and prevent nearby enemies from drawing the whole group across the map.

## Attack timing

- Hydralisk Den, Lurker Den and morph time make this a later attack. With healthy larva and gas income, the first 4-Lurker mixed army should normally form around 8:00–10:00.
- Use Zergling–Roach groups only for defense and exposed targets during the technology transition. Begin the first Lurker attack after at least 4 Lurkers, 8 Hydralisks and 6 Roaches have gathered.
- Keep the Lurkers behind the Roach screen and reinforce with Hydralisks and Zerglings. If fewer than 2 Lurkers or 4 Roaches survive, retreat and rebuild the protective layers before the next attack.
