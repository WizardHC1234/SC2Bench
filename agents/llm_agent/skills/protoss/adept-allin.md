# Adept All-in

A reference for a 1-base Adept all-in. Adjust it dynamically according to the game situation.

## Summary

4 Gateways, Warp Gate, and Adepts. Move out above 10 Adepts. Zealots only spend leftover minerals. No expansion.

## Details

* Opening: 1 Pylon, Probes to 14, 1 Gateway, Probes to 16, 1 Assimilator, Probes to 17, a 2nd Gateway, Probes to 20.
* Tech: Cybernetics Core as soon as the 1st Gateway is ready. When the Core is ready, make 2 Adepts, research Warp Gate, then Adepts toward 100. At 200 minerals after the Core exists, grow to 4 Gateways.
* Chrono: Chronoboost Probe production until 20 Probes, then chronoboost the Warp Gate research.
* Minerals: If gas income is below 25 and minerals reach 200, Zealots toward 100 are the mineral dump. Keep Pylons ahead of supply. Stay on 1 base.
* Attack: Move out once more than 10 Adepts are ready, and reinforce with later Adepts.
