# Disruptors

A reference for a Robotics Bay game built around Disruptors. Adjust it dynamically according to the game situation.

## Summary

2 Gateways into a Robotics Facility and a Robotics Bay, then Disruptors with a Stalker army. Move out as soon as 1 Disruptor exists. Probes grow to 44 on 2 bases.

## Details

* Opening: 1 Pylon, 2 Gateways with priority, 2 Assimilators, a Cybernetics Core, and a Robotics Facility. Research Warp Gate.
* Tech: Robotics Bay with priority, then the natural, and 4 Assimilators. 1 Immortal and 1 Observer before the Disruptors.
* Production: Probes to 22 on 1 base and 44 after the natural. A 3rd Gateway at 300 minerals, and Gateways to 6 when the natural is ready. Disruptors to 4 with priority. Stalkers fill the rest of the warp-ins. Keep Pylons ahead of supply after the 1st Pylon.
* Attack: As soon as 1 Disruptor exists, send it with the Stalkers and the Immortal, and keep reinforcing that army.
