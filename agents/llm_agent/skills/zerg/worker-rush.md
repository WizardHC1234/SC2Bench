# Worker-Rush Follow-Up

## Strategy

The opening calls for direct Drone pressure, followed by a Zergling-flood economy and an optional Mutalisk transition. Keep the Drones mining because workers cannot be assigned to an army group; begin the controllable pressure with the first Zerglings and preserve the intended follow-up.

## Development

- Build an early Spawning Pool, produce the first 4 Zerglings and then take the natural.
- Add the third Hatchery around 24 Drones and the fourth around 30. Grow through roughly 35 toward 40 Drones while maintaining pressure units.
- Begin with 2 Queens and grow to about 4 as bases are added.
- Start with 1 Extractor after the natural, then reach 3 total Extractors for the Lair–Spire transition.
- Research Zergling speed and keep Overlords ahead of repeated Zergling rounds.

## Production and combat

- Continue Zerglings as the main ground pressure after the first group of 4.
- If the ground pressure stalls, produce about 10 Mutalisks from the Spire and use them to attack exposed economy while Zerglings remain active on the ground.
- Gather reinforcements into useful waves and continue the same objective; do not send each new Zergling pair alone.

## Attack timing

- Because workers cannot join the army group, begin controllable pressure with 6 idle Zerglings before 9:00. From 9:00–13:00 gather about 12, from 13:00–17:00 about 20, and afterward about 25 before launching a fresh ground attack.
- Reinforce an active wave while at least half of it survives. When the ground attack is contained, preserve the next Zergling wave and let the first 6–10 Mutalisks open a second pressure route.
