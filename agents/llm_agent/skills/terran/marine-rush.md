# Marine Rush

A reference for a 1-base Marine all-in. Adjust it dynamically according to the game situation.

## Summary

Mineral-only economy, workers stopped at 20 SCVs, 6 Barracks making Marines. Cross the map around 20 Marines and keep streaming more. The plan is to end the game early.

## Details

* Opening: At 14 supply, build 1 Supply Depot. When it is ready, build the 1st Barracks, then a 2nd Supply Depot, and scale straight to 6 Barracks.
* Economy: Take no gas and no 2nd base. Train SCVs only to 20, then put every mineral into Marines. Morph the Command Center to an Orbital Command once the 1st Barracks is ready, and spend spare energy on MULEs. After 5:00, a scan is reasonable when you need to see the enemy before an attack.
* Scouting: Send 1 SCV out after the 1st Supply Depot.
* Army: Marines are the only combat unit. Keep producing them, up toward 200 if the game somehow continues.
* Attack: Gather at the natural and move out once about 20 Marines are ready. Later Marines reinforce that same attack. If a Force Field blocks the enemy ramp, pull back to the natural and go again when the path is open. Near 190 supply, attack with what you have.
