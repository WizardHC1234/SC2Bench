# Rusty Tank

A reference for a gas-first Marine and Siege Tank timing, with Medivacs and a reaction to air. Adjust it dynamically according to the game situation.

## Summary

Expand only after the 1st Marine. 2 Hellions bridge into Tanks, then Marines, Medivacs, and Vikings if the opponent is air. The late shape is about 20 Tanks, 100 Marines, and up to 6 Medivacs, on about 44 SCVs.

## Details

* Opening: Supply Depot at 13, the 1st Refinery at 16, then the Barracks. A 2nd Supply Depot while the Barracks is still building, and the 2nd Refinery at 20 supply. Expand to the natural only after the 1st Marine exists. Morph Orbitals and use MULEs.
* Tanks: Factory and a Tech Lab after the Barracks. Up to 2 Hellions before that Lab is ready, then Siege Tanks. A 2nd Factory after the 1st Tank has started, 4 Refineries, and a Tech Lab on the 2nd Factory. Tanks run toward 20.
* Bio and Medivacs: Barracks to 2, a Starport, then Barracks to 5 with 1 Tech Lab for Combat Shield and 3 Reactors. A Reactor on the Starport. Medivacs in steps of 2, then 4, then 6. Marines: 2 early, then toward 100 once about 250 minerals are banked.
* Air reaction: Vikings to 1, then 3, then 10 if the opponent is playing air. An Engineering Bay, Missile Turrets, and up to 2 Ravens if cloaked units appear.
* Economy: SCVs to 22, then 44. The 3rd base comes after the Barracks and Factory block is already up.
* Attack: Move out once Tanks plus a Marine screen are the main army. The caps above are production targets, not a reason to wait. Keep reinforcing that attack. Near 190 supply, attack with what you have.
