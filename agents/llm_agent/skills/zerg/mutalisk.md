# Mutalisks

A reference for a Spire opening that makes Mutalisks the army. Adjust it dynamically according to the game situation.

## Summary

3 bases into a Lair and a Spire, then Mutalisks with no cap. 16 Zerglings are the early defense. Attack once Mutalisks exist.

## Details

* Opening: An Overlord at 14 Drones, the natural at 16 supply, and Queens to 2. The 1st Extractor when the natural exists. The 2nd Extractor at 4:00, the 3rd at Lair, 5 Extractors once gas is being spent, and 8 when worker supply is about 50.
* Bases: A 3rd base at 30 Drones. A 4th base once a Spire exists.
* Army: 16 Zerglings once. Roaches are fine only until the Spire is ready; after that, stop them. Mutalisks with no cap after the Spire. Inject, and build Overlords before you supply block.
* Attack: Attack with Mutalisks once they exist, and reinforce that group as more finish. There is no large count to wait for.
