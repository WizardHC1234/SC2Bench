# Marine Rush

## Strategy

Use a one-base, mineral-heavy Marine rush. The first attack begins when about 20 living Marines are available; keep replacing losses so pressure does not stop after the first fight. This plan deliberately avoids Refineries and expansion during the rush.

## Development

- Build the first Supply Depot around 13 supply, then add the first Barracks and morph the Command Center into an Orbital Command when possible.
- Stop at about 20 SCVs. Keep enough mineral income to run the Barracks continuously and call MULEs when available.
- Grow to 3 Barracks quickly, then to 6 total. Add Reactors as they become affordable so the same production base can sustain the attack.
- Build Supply Depots ahead of demand. A supply block directly interrupts the only production track in this strategy.

## Production and combat

- Produce Marines continuously. Treat 20 Marines as the first attack force, not as the final army limit.
- Send the first group once roughly 20 living Marines have gathered. Reinforce the same attack with newly produced Marines instead of repeatedly creating small, disconnected waves.
- If the attack is repelled, rebuild a useful group before recommitting. The strategy wins through production tempo and continued pressure rather than a single all-in command.

## Attack timing

- A Marine takes about 18 seconds, and the build grows to 6 Barracks with Reactors. Continuous parallel production should therefore reach 20 Marines around 4:00–5:00; a much later attack indicates idle Barracks or a supply block.
- The first attack leaves with about 20 living Marines. Continue sending reinforcements to that army while at least 12 Marines remain and the enemy position is still vulnerable.
- If the force falls below 12 Marines without breaking the opponent, stop feeding small groups and rebuild to about 20 before the next attack.
