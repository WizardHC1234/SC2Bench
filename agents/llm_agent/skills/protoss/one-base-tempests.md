# One-Base Tempests

A reference for a 1-base Tempest all-in. Adjust it dynamically according to the game situation.

## Summary

No expansion. Stargate, Fleet Beacon, a 2nd Stargate, and Tempests toward 100. Move out when the 1st Tempest exists. Probes stop around 22.

## Details

* Opening: 1 Pylon, Probes to 14, 1 Gateway, Probes to 15, 1 Forge, 2 Assimilators, Probes to 18, a 2nd Pylon, a Cybernetics Core, Probes to 22. Stay on 1 Nexus.
* Air: Stargate, Fleet Beacon when the Stargate is ready, then a 2nd Stargate once the Fleet Beacon exists.
* Army: Tempests toward 100, with priority. Keep Pylons ahead of supply.
* Attack: When the 1st Tempest exists, send it with whatever Gateway units you already have, and reinforce with later Tempests.
