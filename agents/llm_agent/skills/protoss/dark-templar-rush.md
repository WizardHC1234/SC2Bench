# Dark Templar Rush

## Strategy

Reach a Dark Shrine quickly through Gateway, Cybernetics Core and Twilight Council, then send an initial group of 4 Dark Templar. Stalkers and later Zealots keep the plan functional after detection appears.

## Development

- Open with a Gateway and Cybernetics Core, then build the Twilight Council and Dark Shrine without adding unrelated technology first.
- Grow the economy in stages from roughly 14 to 22 and then 30 Probes. Take the natural when the main base begins to run short on accessible minerals and the tech route is secure.
- Scale to 5 Gateways and later 6, supported by 3 total Assimilators and enough Pylons for uninterrupted warp-ins.
- Research Warp Gate. Add Blink for the Stalker support and Charge when Zealots become a larger part of the follow-up.

## Production and combat

- Use 1 early Zealot for basic safety, then add Stalkers while the Dark Shrine is being built.
- Produce 4 Dark Templar for the first strike. Target exposed workers, technology or isolated bases, and preserve survivors when detection arrives.
- Maintain at least a small Stalker group, beginning around 3, so the army can fight units that Dark Templar cannot safely reach.
- Spend excess minerals on Zealots while continuing the gas-heavy units. Do not continue pure Dark Templar production into established detection; transition the Gateway army instead.

## Attack timing

- Four Dark Templar can be produced in one warp-in cycle after the Dark Shrine and Warp Gate are ready, so the first strike should normally occur around 5:00–6:00. Do not wait for the slower Stalker follow-up before starting that covert attack.
- Send the first 4 Dark Templar together as soon as they are ready, splitting them only when more than one exposed mineral line or technology target is known.
- Start the follow-up frontal pressure after at least 4 Dark Templar and 6 Stalkers are alive. If detection makes the Dark Templar ineffective, preserve them and rebuild the visible Gateway force to at least 8 combat units before recommitting.
