# Lurkers

A reference for a hatch-first game that techs to Lurkers. Adjust it dynamically according to the game situation.

## Summary

Standard expand, then a Hydralisk Den into a Lurker Den. Hydralisks are the body of the army, Lurkers the power, with the range upgrade. Attack once Lurkers are in the army.

## Details

* Opening: An Overlord at 14 Drones, the natural at 16 supply, and the 1st Extractor at 20 supply. 4 Zerglings for defense, a Spawning Pool, then Queens. Inject throughout.
* Bases: A 3rd base at 3:00, a 4th when worker supply is about 40. Gases climb from 2 up to 8 as the Lurker tech comes online.
* Tech: Hydralisk Den, then morph it into a Lurker Den. Research Lurker range. Hydralisks are the body; morph Lurkers from them as gas allows. Build Overlords before you supply block.
* Attack: Move out once Lurkers exist, with the Hydralisks beside them, and keep reinforcing that group.
