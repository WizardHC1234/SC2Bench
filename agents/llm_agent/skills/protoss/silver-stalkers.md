# Silver Stalker Macro

## Strategy

Play a stable two-base Stalker build: reach 44 Probes, 7 Gateways and 4 Assimilators with Warp Gate reinforcement. The plan favors a broad, repeatable macro structure over a precise all-in timing.

## Development

- Open Pylon, Assimilator and Gateway, then grow toward about 20 Probes and take the natural Nexus.
- Add the Cybernetics Core and second Assimilator, followed by continued Probe production toward roughly 44.
- Reach 4 total Assimilators and about 7 Gateways as the two-base economy matures.
- Complete Warp Gate and add a Twilight Council as the next technology structure. This build does not commit to a particular Twilight upgrade. Keep Pylon capacity ahead of repeated Gateway production.

## Production and combat

- Produce Stalkers continuously as the main army. The high unit target means sustained replacement production, not one oversized request.
- Hold the natural until a coherent Stalker group exists, then pressure exposed zones, preserve damaged units and reinforce the active army through Warp Gates.
- If an attacked location is cleared, reassign the group to a current objective or search unconfirmed expansions instead of leaving the main force permanently idle.

## Attack timing

- The 15-Stalker force should normally form around 6:30–7:30 as the Gateway count grows. If it is substantially later, move with 10–12 gathered Stalkers once the bases are safe instead of missing the entire pressure window.
- Begin the first main attack at about 15 living Stalkers. This is large enough to focus targets and absorb initial losses without delaying the two-base pressure too long.
- Reinforce the same group through Warp Gates. If the force falls below about 10 Stalkers, regroup before taking another defended position; use a smaller surviving group only for scouting or intercepting exposed units.
