# Burrow Roach Pressure

## Strategy

Use a compact two-base Roach opening, research Burrow early and preserve damaged Roaches by letting badly injured units burrow and recover. Ravagers are added in response to enemy Siege Tanks and Photon Cannons.

## Development

- Produce an Overlord around 13 Drones, take the natural near 16 Drones, then add the first Extractor and Spawning Pool around 17 supply.
- Build the Roach Warren near 24 supply, take the second Extractor and grow to about 25 Drones.
- Produce the first Queen and then a second. Keep Overlords ahead of the Roach production rounds.
- Research Burrow once about 100 gas is available.

## Production and combat

- Produce about 6 early Zerglings for safety, then the first 5 Roaches. Continue Roaches as the main army and replace losses.
- Use the number of visible enemy Siege Tanks and Photon Cannons as a guide for Ravager morphs; add Ravagers to break those positions instead of using a fixed count in every game.
- Severely damaged Roaches will burrow and return after recovering. Avoid committing the group so deeply that this recovery behavior cannot save it.
- Attack with a gathered Roach force and keep new Roaches reinforcing the same objective.

## Attack timing

- The Roaches themselves train quickly, but Burrow research takes a little over a minute. The first 5-Roach pressure should normally begin around 4:30–5:30, without waiting for every later Roach to finish.
- Begin the first attack with the initial 5 Roaches and about 6 Zerglings. Do not send the Zerglings ahead to fight alone; their purpose is to screen for the Roaches.
- Reinforce the same pressure with new Roaches and the Ravagers justified by visible defenses. If fewer than 3 Roaches survive, retreat, recover the damaged units and rebuild to at least 5 before attacking again.
