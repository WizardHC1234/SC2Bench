# Ling Flood

A reference for a hatch-first economy that becomes a Zergling flood. Adjust it dynamically according to the game situation.

## Summary

Expand first, drone toward 30, then Zerglings with no cap. The 1st attack leaves once about 12 Lings exist, and production never stops.

## Details

* Opening: At 14 Drones, make the 2nd Overlord and expand to the natural. Drones to 20, then 1 Queen, then Drones to 30. A Spawning Pool goes down with the natural.
* Bases: A 3rd base at 24 Drones, a 4th at 30. 1 Queen per new base, and Queens toward 10 if minerals reach 500. Inject every base that has a Queen.
* Gas: The 1st Extractor once the natural exists. Later gases are not part of the early flood.
* Army: 12 Zerglings once the Pool is ready. After 30 Drones, Zerglings with no cap. Build Overlords before you supply block.
* Attack: Send the Lings once the 1st 12 exist, and keep adding new Lings to the same attack. Keep this as continuous production.
