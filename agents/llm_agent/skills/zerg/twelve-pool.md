# Twelve Pool

A reference for a 12-pool Zergling rush. Adjust it dynamically according to the game situation.

## Summary

The Spawning Pool comes before anything else. Drone only to 14, then Zerglings with no cap. The attack leaves immediately, can take up to 10 nearby Drones with it, and does not come home.

## Details

* Opening: 1 Spawning Pool before any other building. When it exists, the 2nd Overlord and Drones to 14. Another Overlord whenever supply left is 0.
* Army: Zerglings with no cap. Inject if a Queen exists, but the Lings come first; a Queen is not part of the rush itself.
* Attack: Send Lings as soon as they exist. Drones that are already near the enemy, up to about 10, can join. Keep the attack going.
* If the rush is over: only once enemy buildings are flying and you are above 30 supply, take 2 Extractors, expand, Drones to 20, morph a Lair, Drones to 30, 4 Extractors, a Spire, and Mutalisks to 10.
