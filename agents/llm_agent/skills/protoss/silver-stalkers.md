# Silver Stalkers

A reference for a simple Stalker macro. Adjust it dynamically according to the game situation.

## Summary

The same 2-base Stalker plan as a standard macro, plus an optional Twilight Council. Stalkers toward 100. Attack once 4 Gateways are producing, on about 44 Probes.

## Details

* Opening: Probes to 14, 1 Pylon, Probes to 16, 1 Assimilator, 1 Gateway, Probes to 20, then the natural.
* Tech: Cybernetics Core, Probes to 21, the 2nd gas, Probes to 22, another Pylon, and Warp Gate.
* Scale: Probes to 44 after the 2nd Nexus. A 3rd Assimilator unless gas is 300. A Twilight Council is optional and comes before the extra Gateways if you build it. Then Gateways to 7, and a 4th Assimilator unless gas is 200. Stalkers toward 100. Keep Pylons ahead of supply.
* Attack: Move out once 4 Gateways are producing, and reinforce with later Stalkers. 100 is the production cap, not a reason to wait.
