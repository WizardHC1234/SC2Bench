# Roach Rush

A reference for a 1-base Roach rush. Adjust it dynamically according to the game situation.

## Summary

A short opening, then Roaches without a pause and without a 2nd base. Send every Roach at the nearest enemy building.

## Details

* Opening, in this order: 1 Drone, a Spawning Pool, 2 Drones, 1 Extractor, 1 Drone, 1 Overlord, a Roach Warren, 1 Queen, 1 Overlord.
* After that list: inject with the Queen, and put 3 Drones on the Extractor. Then an Overlord whenever supply is about to block, and Roaches continuously. Stay on 1 base for the rush.
* Attack: Send every Roach and Zergling at the nearest enemy building, or at the enemy main if none are visible. Keep that army on the attack. The rush continues until no Drones remain to restart it.
