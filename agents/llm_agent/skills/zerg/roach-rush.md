# One-Base Roach Rush

## Strategy

Stay on one base and reach Roaches as quickly as possible. Gas supports Roaches; excess minerals become Zerglings, Overlords and a small worker economy. The first Roaches move out immediately rather than waiting for a large wave.

## Opening

- Begin with 1 Drone, then the Spawning Pool, followed by 2 more Drones.
- Build exactly 1 Extractor, add another Drone and then an Overlord. Do not add a second Extractor during the one-base rush.
- As soon as the Pool is ready, start the Roach Warren and 1 Queen. Add the next Overlord before Roach production becomes supply blocked.
- Do not take an expansion during the rush.

## Production and combat

- Produce a Roach whenever the larvae, supply and resources allow it.
- After the first Roach, add Drones only while below roughly 16 workers; the attack remains the priority.
- When gas is insufficient for another Roach, spend spare minerals and larvae on Zerglings without delaying the next Roach round.
- Maintain Overlord capacity ahead of demand. Use the Queen for larva support so the one-base production can continue.
- Move the first Roaches toward the enemy while the opening group gathers, then keep reinforcing the same attack. Keep this army to Roaches and Zerglings and use it against ground targets.

## Attack timing

- Roaches train in about 19 seconds and share larvae with the Zerglings and Drones. Reserve enough larvae for the attack so the 5-Roach group normally moves out around 3:30–4:30.
- The first Roach may move across the map to preserve the rush timing, but begin the committed attack after 5 Roaches and about 6 Zerglings have gathered. Earlier units should avoid fighting alone while the rest catch up.
- Send each new Roach to reinforce that attack. If fewer than 3 Roaches survive and the enemy entrance remains defended, regroup to at least 5 rather than continuing with isolated replacements.
