# Dark Templar

A reference for a Dark Templar rush that continues into Stalkers. Adjust it dynamically according to the game situation.

## Summary

Dark Shrine as fast as the tech allows, about 4 Dark Templars as the 1st strike, then Stalkers. Attack once that small cloaked force is out, around the strength of a 20-supply army.

## Details

* Opening: At 14 supply, 1 Pylon. The 1st Assimilator and 1 Gateway at 16 supply, then the 2nd Assimilator. A 2nd Pylon at 21 supply, and a 2nd Gateway.
* Tech: Cybernetics Core as soon as the 1st Gateway is ready, Twilight Council when the Core is ready, Dark Shrine when the Council is ready. Warp Gate from the Core. A 3rd Gateway immediately after. Blink and Charge once the Council is free. Chronoboost the key research.
* Economy: Probes to 14, then 22. Expand when the main minerals are running low. After the natural, Probes to 30, Gateways to 6, and a 3rd Assimilator.
* Army: Dark Templars to 4 with priority. 1 Zealot, then a few Stalkers, then continuous Stalkers. More Zealots when minerals reach about 400.
* Attack: Send the Dark Templars first, with Stalkers as they finish, and keep reinforcing that attack.
