# Void Rays

A reference for a macro that makes Void Rays the priority. Adjust it dynamically according to the game situation.

## Summary

2 bases, a 3rd around 5:00, then Stargates. Void Rays toward 20 are the priority. Zealots and Adepts are the ground army. Attack once the 1st Void Rays are out.

## Details

* Opening: 1 Pylon, Probes to 14, then 16, 1 Assimilator, 1 Gateway, Probes to 20, a Cybernetics Core, Probes to 21, the natural, Probes to 22, the 2nd Assimilator, and another Pylon. Research Warp Gate. 2 Stalkers with priority.
* Economy: Probes to 44 on 2 bases and 56 on 3. The 3rd base comes at 5:00. Skip a new Assimilator when you already hold about 300 gas early, or about 200 gas later.
* Tech: Twilight Council, 1 Stargate, Charge, and Resonating Glaives. A 2nd Stargate and Gateways to 4.
* Army: Void Rays toward 20 with priority. On the ground, the steps are Zealots to 6, Adepts to 10, Zealots to 15, Adepts to 20, Zealots to 23, and Adepts to 30.
* Attack: Move out once the 1st Void Rays are with a small Gateway force, and keep adding both to the same army. 20 Void Rays is the priority cap, not a reason to wait.
