# Void Ray Macro

## Strategy

Build a two-base Void Ray core, then add a third base around 5:00 and support the air army with Gateway units. The supporting composition grows in stages as the economy expands.

## Development

- Use a standard Gateway–Cybernetics Core opening and take the natural, growing toward about 44 Probes.
- Start a third Nexus around 5:00 when it is defensible and continue toward roughly 56 Probes.
- Take the first 2 Assimilators during the two-base opening, add the third during the initial Stargate phase, and reach 5 total after starting the third Nexus. Build the first Stargate and later a second, while growing to about 4 Gateways.
- Add a Twilight Council, research Warp Gate and Charge, and research Adept Glaives when Adepts are part of the support force.

## Production

- Produce 2 early Stalkers for safety, then build Void Rays continuously toward a long-term ceiling of about 20.
- Add Zealots and Adepts in stages as mineral income rises: first about 6 Zealots and 10 Adepts, then 15 and 20, with later ceilings near 23 Zealots and 30 Adepts.
- These late counts describe how to grow and replace the support army; do not queue the entire composition at once.

## Combat

- Keep Gateway units between ground threats and the Void Rays. Avoid committing the air core into concentrated anti-air without the support army.
- Use the third-base economy to replace losses and continue pressure with a gathered mixed force.

## Attack timing

- Three Void Rays normally become available around 5:30–6:30; the larger mixed army follows around 7:00–8:30. Do not hold the first air group at home until the later Gateway composition is complete.
- Begin air pressure with 3 Void Rays, supported by at least 2 surviving Stalkers. This is enough to threaten an exposed target without risking the first expensive ship by itself.
- Start a frontal mixed attack once about 6 Void Rays and 8 Gateway units are alive. If concentrated anti-air destroys half of the Void Ray group, disengage and rebuild or change the supporting Gateway composition before recommitting.
