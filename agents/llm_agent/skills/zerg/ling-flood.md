# Zergling Flood

## Strategy

Use early Zerglings for recurring pressure while expanding Hatchery and Drone production behind them. Follow either the speedling–roach route or the speedling–Mutalisk route according to the available economy and enemy army instead of trying to build both at once.

## Development

- Take the natural early, then build the first Extractor and Spawning Pool. Grow from roughly 20 Drones toward 30 and then 40 while keeping enough larvae for each pressure wave.
- Add the third Hatchery around 24 Drones and the fourth around 30 when the map position is safe.
- Begin with about 2 Queens, then grow to 3–4. Add more only when the Hatcheries and mineral economy can use them.
- Research Zergling speed as the first upgrade.

## Army paths

- Speedling–roach path: remain on 1 Extractor, form early groups of about 12 and then 16 Zerglings, add 4 Roaches and later around 10, then continue Zerglings as the main pressure unit.
- Speedling–Mutalisk path: make the first 4 Zerglings before the natural, start with 1 Extractor and reach 3 total for the Lair–Spire transition, produce about 10 Mutalisks, and keep Zerglings as the ground army.
- Do not reserve all larvae for one unit type while Drone or Overlord production is immediately needed.

## Combat

- Before 4:00, a group of about 6 idle Zerglings can begin pressure; from 4:00–7:00 use roughly 12, from 7:00–10:00 about 20, and later around 25 before sending a fresh attack.
- Use Zerglings, Roaches and Queens against ground pressure. Mutalisks provide the air response and harassment in the Spire branch.

## Attack timing

- Before 4:00, attack with about 6 idle Zerglings; from 4:00–7:00 gather about 12, from 7:00–10:00 about 20, and afterward about 25 before starting a fresh wave.
- New Zerglings reinforce an active attack while at least half of its starting group survives. After heavier losses, collect the next full wave instead of sending each larva pair separately.
