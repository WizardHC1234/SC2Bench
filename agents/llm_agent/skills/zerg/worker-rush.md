# Worker Rush

A reference for a Drone rush with a Zergling flood behind it. Adjust it dynamically according to the game situation.

## Summary

24 Drones leave for the enemy main immediately. A Pool, an expansion, and Zerglings are built behind that attack. Gas mining returns to normal at 70 supply or once a Lair exists.

## Details

* Rush: Train Drones to 24 and send them at the enemy main at once. The Drones are the 1st attack, and they leave before any Lings exist.
* Behind it: a Spawning Pool, the natural, Zergling speed, Queens, and injections. Zerglings as soon as the Pool is ready. Pull Drones off gas once you have 100 gas, or once Zergling speed has started.
* Later: at 70 supply, or once a Lair exists, mine gas normally again and continue into the later army. Overlords before you supply block.
* Attack: The Drone attack leaves immediately. Lings and later combat units reinforce that same attack.
