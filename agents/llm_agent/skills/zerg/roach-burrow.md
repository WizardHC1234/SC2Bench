# Roach Burrow

A reference for an early 2-base Roach attack built around burrow and Roach speed. Adjust it dynamically according to the game situation.

## Summary

Natural at 16 Drones, a Roach Warren, burrow, and Roach speed. Move out with a small Roach force. Drone only far enough to pay for 2 bases.

## Details

* Opening: Expand at 16 Drones. The 1st Extractor, a Spawning Pool, a Roach Warren, then the 2nd Extractor.
* Upgrades: Research burrow, and Roach speed from the Warren. Once burrow is researched, the Roaches use it on their own.
* Economy: Queens on the bases, and inject. Drones only far enough to support 2 bases and the Warren. Overlords before you supply block.
* Army: Roaches are the army.
* Attack: Move out once a small Roach force exists, and keep reinforcing it.
