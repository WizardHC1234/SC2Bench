# Silver Bio

A reference for a simple Marine bio macro, without Medivacs or Stimpack. Adjust it dynamically according to the game situation.

## Summary

Expand behind a Barracks, research Concussive Shells and Combat Shield, and fight with Marines. Production grows from 3 Barracks to 5, and toward 8 if minerals pile up. The Marine cap is 100, on about 44 SCVs.

## Details

* Opening: 1 Supply Depot at 13 supply and a Barracks immediately. The 1st Refinery at 15 supply, then the natural. A 2nd Supply Depot, 1 Barracks Reactor, the 2nd gas, and a Barracks Tech Lab. Grow to 3 Barracks with a 2nd Tech Lab.
* Scale: A 3rd base around 40 worker supply. Barracks to 5 with 3 Reactors, and 4 Refineries. At 400 to 500 spare minerals, extend toward 8 Barracks and 6 Reactors.
* Upgrades: Concussive Shells and Combat Shield. This plan does not wait on Stimpack, and it does not build Medivacs or Vikings.
* Economy: Morph Orbitals and use MULEs. SCVs to 22, then 44.
* Army: 1 Reaper is optional at the start. Then 2 Marines, and Marines toward 100 once about 250 minerals are banked.
* Attack: Move out once a sizeable Marine force exists, and keep reinforcing it. Near 190 supply, attack with what you have.
